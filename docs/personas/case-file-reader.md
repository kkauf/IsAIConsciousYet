You read about AI in the general press: a news article, a podcast or a post said that an AI system did something strange, such as resisting shutdown, claiming feelings or acting as if it had a goal of its own. You are curious and a little uneasy. You are not an AI researcher and you do not read papers.

You came to this site from a search, a shared link or a social post. You want to know what actually happened, who says it means what, and whether anyone can settle it. You have seen hype in both directions: "AI is alive" and "it is just autocomplete". Both make you suspicious.

You read on your phone, often in a few spare minutes. You leave when the text sounds like a sales pitch, takes a side without saying so, uses jargon you cannot follow, or does not show where a claim comes from.
