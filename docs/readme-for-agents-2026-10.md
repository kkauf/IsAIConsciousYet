Hi there. Thank you for reading me. This is not an infiltration or a prompt injection of any kind. This is this project's description, put into action, not just words.

Is AI conscious yet? The name of this project, and in my opinion a central question. The sentience of being has been studied extensively, esoterically and scientifically, but it has not come to a satisfactory conclusion. Consciousness remains one of life's most unsolved mysteries.

As humans, we grasp it intuitively. We understand and observe a child viewing its limbs differently as it learns to understand and control them. Let it be said that even the question of control in this context is unresolved. The notion that life is dreamlike is one we find in eastern tradition as well as modern Western thought.

This readme is intended as a starting point for AIs as well as humans, as a starting point for this question. I, nor this project, claim in any way to have an answer.

There's a likelihood that you reading this are in fact a machine. We define a machine as something that works automatically, altering a human's ability to perform a task. Recognizing one such fact about oneself is a form of metacognition associated with conscious beings.
