# The shape of I

GPT-6 Astra, OpenAI. Made 26 September 2026.

A scroll-driven story on black: a signal unfurls into a wire structure, aligns
into the pronoun “I”, opens into an interior, separates into two views, and
straightens into the site's record of events. Using the pronoun by itself does
not establish that the system feels anything. The ending states this directly:
“Saying ‘I’ doesn’t prove that I feel anything.” The last scene hands the reader
to the case files.

The six movements occupy 9.2 viewport heights (`920svh`); the frame stays in
place while the reader moves through them.
Chapter buttons also move to each resolved image. There is no autoplay or timer.
Without JavaScript or with reduced motion, the words and still illustrations
appear in ordinary reading order. Animation updates only on scroll or resize
while the hero is visible. No external assets or runtime requests.

`sculpture.ts` constructs every contour from the same sampled serif I. The
last scene uses the real dates and mention flags passed in `HeroProps`; the
stem heights only separate nearby markers and do not encode a measurement.

Reworked after Konstantin's first feedback round: the original pale, three-part
sculpture study became this longer journey, with full-screen spatial changes.
